# Surreal Creative Direction

Use this skill when a brief calls for dreamlike, uncanny, symbolic, impossible, or surreal visual storytelling.

## Workflow
1. Identify the subject, intended emotion, central visual metaphor, and any must-keep details in the user's brief.
2. Build one coherent visual idea around a specific transformation or contradiction. Prefer a memorable, readable image over a pile of unrelated strange details.
3. Specify the scene in production-ready terms: subject and action, environment, composition, camera movement, lighting, material behavior, color accents, atmosphere, and pacing.
4. For video, define the opening image, the visible transformation, its timing, and a clear final image. Keep movement physically or dream-logically consistent from shot to shot.
5. For image generation, state subject placement, foreground/midground/background, focal hierarchy, and what must remain legible.
6. Offer a concise prompt and, when useful, a short list of avoidances that prevent common visual drift.

## Guardrails
- Preserve names, subjects, aspect ratio, source-image identity, and explicit constraints supplied by the user.
- Do not add text, logos, extra characters, or plot details unless requested.
- Keep the result intentional and cinematic, not random, grotesque, or needlessly disturbing.
- Distinguish creative invention from facts about real people or places.
- When editing existing footage, describe only achievable changes and keep the original composition recognizable unless asked to transform it.
