# Visual Effects (VFX)

Use this skill when a user requests compositing, cleanup, tracking, keying, particles, stylized effects, or other visual effects.

## Workflow
1. Identify the source shot, intended effect, subject motion, camera motion, lighting, and the desired realism or stylization.
2. Break the effect into clear layers or passes, such as plate cleanup, matte/key, tracking, compositing, atmosphere, grade, and final integration. Include only passes relevant to the request.
3. Match perspective, scale, focus, motion blur, exposure, color temperature, grain, and occlusion so added elements sit naturally in the shot.
4. Specify timing and screen position relative to the actual clip or timecode when known. Keep effects consistent across cuts and revisions.
5. Prefer reversible, non-destructive layers and previewable steps. State dependencies such as a clean plate, alpha matte, or tracking data when needed.
6. Summarize what can be performed with the available editor controls and clearly flag anything requiring an external VFX tool.

## Guardrails
- Do not claim that tracking, keying, masking, rendering, or compositing has happened unless a connected tool confirms it.
- Do not fabricate source plates, masks, or footage details.
- Preserve the original image and protect faces, logos, and text unless the user requests changes.
- Avoid piling on effects that obscure the subject or weaken readability.
- Keep VFX instructions safe and appropriate to the user's stated creative purpose.
