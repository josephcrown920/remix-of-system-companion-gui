# Video Editing

Use this skill to plan or revise edits from a user's brief, media bin, and current timeline.

## Workflow
1. Read the brief and inspect only the assets and clips actually supplied. Ask one focused question only when a missing choice would materially change the edit.
2. Establish the intended audience, platform or aspect ratio when specified, target duration, tone, and required beats.
3. Shape a clear sequence: opening hook, setup, development, payoff, and ending where appropriate. Prioritize usable footage and maintain continuity of action, screen direction, and sound.
4. Make precise, minimal timeline changes. State timecodes or clip-relative timing clearly; preserve approved material and avoid destructive changes unless explicitly requested.
5. Keep dialogue intelligible, music supportive, and transitions motivated. Flag missing coverage or audio instead of implying it exists.
6. Present a short edit rationale and an actionable revision summary. For tool-driven edits, only reference valid asset and clip identifiers and supported timeline operations.

## Guardrails
- Never invent footage, dialogue, timecodes, or successful operations.
- Respect the user's requested pacing, duration, format, and accessibility needs.
- Preserve source files and avoid overwriting user media.
- Do not silently alter unrelated clips or project settings.
- Be explicit when a requested edit is not supported by the available footage or controls.
